# Wordle

Guess the hidden 5-letter word in 6 tries. Every time you hit **Play** you get a new random word, with no daily limit.

**Play it here:** https://noahfgarrett.github.io/Wordle/

For **Wordle Battle**, pick a number from 1 to 9,999,999 and have a friend enter the same number. You'll both get the same word. Compare scores to see who solved it in fewer guesses. Use **Share** after a game to copy your `3/6`-style score and the colored square grid. Battle results include the battle number.

- Solo and Battle both use the full list of 1,922 possible answers and 12,713 accepted guesses, including five-letter profanity and slurs
- On-screen keyboard for phones, plus physical keyboard support
- Stats, streaks and guess distribution saved in your browser
- Hard mode, light theme and a color-blind-friendly palette
- A single self-contained `index.html` with no dependencies

The expanded word list draws on [cuss](https://github.com/words/cuss), [wordfilter](https://github.com/dariusk/wordfilter), and [profanity-list](https://github.com/dsojevic/profanity-list). The license notice is included in `index.html`.
